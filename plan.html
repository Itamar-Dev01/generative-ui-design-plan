A design plan, not code. Each decision shows two or three structurally different directions as live mockups. Pick an option and the screen switches in place. Press Respond to copy a structured design brief. Answers stay in this browser; nothing is sent.

Why · source and scope If a library component fits, I fill its schema. If not, I build the UI on the fly. Mode 2: when no component fits, the model emits a UINode tree from the primitive vocabulary.

The demo works. Its shell does not have a design yet. This plan fixes the design decisions first. The proposed directions are options, not an approved build.
